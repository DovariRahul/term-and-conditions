1Clause 01

Purpose of the App

RVR Blood Bank is designed to help students and authorized members of the college community connect blood donors with people who need blood. The platform facilitates communication between parties and does not operate as a medical service provider.

2Clause 02

Accurate Information

Users must provide correct and up-to-date information at all times, including:

  • Full name and contact details
  • Blood group (verified)
  • Phone number and current availability status
  • Accurate blood request details — quantity, urgency, and hospital name

Providing false information is a violation of these Terms and may result in account suspension.

3Clause 03

Genuine Blood Requests

Users must submit only genuine, verified blood requests. Fake or misleading requests are strictly prohibited. Such conduct diverts attention from real emergencies and may result in immediate account termination.

4Clause 04

Donor Responsibility

Registering as a donor does not certify medical eligibility to donate blood. Donors are solely responsible for:

  • Assessing their own medical fitness before each donation
  • Following appropriate medical advice prior to donating
  • Meeting standard blood donation eligibility criteria

RVR Blood Bank bears no responsibility for health outcomes from donation by a medically unfit donor.

5Clause 05

Medical Disclaimer

This application only facilitates communication. RVR Blood Bank explicitly does not:

  • Provide medical advice of any kind
  • Offer diagnosis or treatment recommendations
  • Guarantee the availability of any blood type
  • Guarantee the safety or suitability of any blood offered

All medical decisions must be made by qualified healthcare professionals.

6Clause 06

Emergency Situations

This app must not be your sole source of help during a medical emergency. In any urgent situation, users must:

  • Immediately contact the nearest hospital or blood bank
  • Call 108 (Ambulance) or 112 (National Emergency)
  • Consult a licensed physician without delay
7Clause 07

Privacy & Personal Information

Users must not misuse another user's personal information — including phone numbers, addresses, blood group, or contact details shared during a request. Misuse may violate applicable Indian data protection law. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

8Clause 08

Medical Documents

Any medical proof uploaded by a requester must be:

  • Genuine and unaltered
  • Used only to verify the blood request
  • Directly relevant to the specific request submitted

Submitting fabricated or stolen documents may result in legal action in addition to account termination.

9Clause 09

Prohibited Activities

The following are strictly forbidden on the RVR Blood Bank platform:

  • Creating fake or duplicate accounts
  • Submitting fraudulent or non-urgent blood requests
  • Uploading false, edited, or stolen medical documents
  • Harassing, threatening, or abusing other users
  • Misusing the platform for commercial or personal gain
  • Sharing another user's personal information without consent
10Clause 10

Admin Rights

The platform administrator may:

  • Verify, approve, or reject any blood request at their discretion
  • Remove or flag any content that violates these Terms
  • Suspend or permanently deactivate accounts found in violation
  • Access activity logs for safety and compliance review

Admin decisions are final and made in the interest of community safety.

11Clause 11

Service Availability

The app may occasionally be unavailable due to scheduled maintenance, technical problems, or network issues. Users must not rely on this app as their only means of accessing blood in a medical situation. Always keep independent contact with local hospitals and blood banks.

12Clause 12

Changes to Terms

These Terms may be updated when necessary. The updated version will be published with a new date and version number. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the latest version. Significant changes will be communicated via in-app notification where possible.

13Clause 13

Acceptance

By creating an account and using RVR Blood Bank, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms & Conditions in their current version.
