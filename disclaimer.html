Medical Disclaimer

Please read carefully. This defines the boundaries of what RVR Blood Bank can and cannot do for you medically.

1Section 01

Not a Medical Service

RVR Blood Bank is a communication and coordination platform — not a medical service provider, healthcare institution, or clinical blood bank. It enables community members to connect for blood donation coordination.

No doctor, nurse, or licensed medical professional is involved in the platform's operation. The platform does not perform, supervise, or take responsibility for any medical procedures.

2Section 02

No Medical Advice

Nothing communicated through RVR Blood Bank constitutes:

  • Medical advice of any kind
  • A medical diagnosis or health assessment
  • A treatment recommendation
  • A clinical endorsement of any blood donation or transfusion
  • A substitute for consultation with a licensed physician

All decisions about blood donation or transfusion must be made by qualified medical professionals.

3Section 03

No Blood Availability Guarantee

RVR Blood Bank does not guarantee:

  • That a donor of any specific blood type will be available at any given time
  • That blood offered by a registered donor is medically safe or suitable
  • That a match will be found within any particular timeframe
  • That a blood request will be fulfilled at all

Users must maintain independent access to hospital blood banks and licensed medical facilities. This platform supplements — it does not replace — those resources.

4Section 04

Donor Medical Fitness

The platform does not screen, assess, or certify whether a registered donor is medically fit to donate blood at any given time. Blood donation eligibility depends on many factors including recent illness, medications, haemoglobin levels, and other medical criteria.

⚠️
Donors: Get Medical Clearance First
Before every donation, confirm your fitness with a doctor or at a certified blood bank. Donating when medically unfit can endanger both you and the recipient.
5Section 05

Emergency Protocol

During any medical emergency, do not rely solely on this application. Follow these steps in order:

1
Call 108 or 112 immediately

Ambulance / National Emergency — do this first, before anything else

2
Transport to the nearest hospital

Go directly to the emergency ward — don't wait

3
Contact the hospital's blood bank directly

They have on-call blood supplies and trained medical staff

4
Use this app as supplementary outreach only

Only after steps 1–3 are already in motion — as an additional community reach

6Section 06

Limitation of Liability

RVR Blood Bank, its administrators, and RVR & JC College of Engineering & Technology shall not be held liable for:

  • Medical outcomes from donations or transfusions coordinated through the app
  • Harm from over-reliance on this app during a medical emergency
  • Any loss resulting from platform unavailability at a critical time
  • The safety or medical suitability of any blood offered by a donor
  • Any third-party actions or communications made through the platform

By using this application, you acknowledge these limitations and agree that all medical decisions are your sole responsibility and that of your treating healthcare provider.
