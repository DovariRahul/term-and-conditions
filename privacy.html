Privacy Policy

How RVR Blood Bank collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information — and what rights you have over your own data.

DPDP Act 2023 Version 2.1 Sept 2026

Your Privacy Matters
We collect only the minimum data needed to connect donors with requesters. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any external commercial entity.

2Section 02

Why We Collect It

  • Name & contact — to enable donors and requesters to coordinate blood donation
  • Blood group — to match compatible donors with blood requests
  • Medical documents — to verify the legitimacy of a blood request (admin only)
  • Activity logs — for security, compliance auditing, and abuse prevention

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the platform's core function.

3Section 03

Who Can See Your Data

  • Other users — may see your name and blood group as a potential donor. Phone number is shared only if you respond to a request.
  • Blood requesters — can view limited donor profile info to facilitate contact
  • Administrators — can access full account data for verification and compliance
  • College authorities — only if required by an official investigation
🚫
We Never Share With Third Parties
We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any external commercial entity — ever.
4Section 04

Data Storage & Security

  • Encrypted storage for sensitive personal and medical information
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only
  • Secure authentication protocols for administrative access
  • Regular security reviews of platform infrastructure
⚠️
No System is 100% Secure
Please do not share sensitive personal or medical information beyond what is strictly necessary for your request or registration.
5Section 05

Data Retention

  • Active accounts — data retained for the duration of college membership
  • Completed requests — retained 2 years for compliance and audit
  • Medical documents — retained 90 days after request closure, then permanently deleted
  • Account deletion — personal data deleted within 30 days of a deletion request
6Section 06

Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data, subject to legal limitations
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format upon request
  • Withdraw consent — which may require account deletion

To exercise any of these rights, contact the platform administrator through official college channels.

7Section 07

Third-Party Services

RVR Blood Bank may use limited third-party services for hosting. These services are selected to be DPDP-compliant. We do not integrate any advertising, analytics, or social media tracking services on this platform.

8Section 08

Contact & Complaints

If you have concerns about your data or wish to exercise your rights:

  • Contact the RVR Blood Bank administrator via the official college portal
  • Raise concerns with the college's IT Department or Data Protection Officer
  • Lodge a complaint with India's Data Protection Board if unresolved
